Only the field props used by the archive world are included: workbenches,
scrolls, ropes, hand tools, buckets, benches, barrels, lanterns, bags, and an
open handcart. The original shared texture atlases were resized to
512px for real-time web delivery. Runtime atlases use WebP: normal maps are
lossless, while color, ORM, and page-noise maps use quality-90 encoding with
lossless alpha. Model geometry, texture resolution, and material assignments
are unchanged.
