This directory contains a deliberately small subset of the original glTF pack:
tree variants, medium rocks, a bush, a fern, short grass, a mushroom, and the
textures referenced by those models. The original models and textures were not
generated inside this repository.

For browser delivery, the original 2048px PNG textures were converted to WebP
at the same dimensions with alpha preserved, and the glTF image references were
updated accordingly. Geometry, materials, UVs, and model hierarchy are unchanged.
