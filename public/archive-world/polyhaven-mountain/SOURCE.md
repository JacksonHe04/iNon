# Poly Haven mountain surfaces

Both materials are distributed by Poly Haven under CC0.

- `rocky_terrain_03_diff_1k.webp`
  - Asset: Rocky Terrain 03
  - Author: Amal Kumar
  - Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_terrain_03
- `snow_02_diff_1k.webp`
  - Asset: Snow 02
  - Author: Rob Tuytel
  - Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/snow_02

The 1K diffuse maps use quality-95 WebP runtime derivatives and are blended
into the collision terrain by elevation and slope. The mountain shape remains
part of the same infinite Rapier trimesh as the rest of the world, so the snow
line is walkable rather than a distant backdrop.
