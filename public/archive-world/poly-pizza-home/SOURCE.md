# Home grounds assets

These assets are stored locally so the homestead is composed from authored 3D
models rather than procedural stand-ins.

- `PublicPool.glb` — **Public Pool** by Armory_3D, CC0, downloaded from
  https://poly.pizza/m/OUA4U30z8A
- `Flowers.glb` — **Flowers** by Quaternius, CC0, downloaded from
  https://poly.pizza/m/NBUxHir6FJ
- `Campfire.glb` — **Campfire** by jeremy, Creative Commons Attribution,
  downloaded from https://poly.pizza/m/dxxHpVXHLZg
- `LogPile.glb` — **Log Pile** by matt bower, Creative Commons Attribution,
  downloaded from https://poly.pizza/m/aSF_EyEAIl0

The assets are used with their original geometry and materials, with a faded
green scene tint applied at runtime to fit the Verdant Archive art direction.
